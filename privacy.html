Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Barometric Pressure Map (“the site”) is a free tool that shows barometric pressure and weather for places around the world. This page explains what information is used when you visit the site, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

The site has no accounts and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal details. We do not run our own analytics or tracking.

Stored on your device only. Your settings (units, theme), favourite and recent places, report details you type in (such as a site name or notes) and cached weather data are saved in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Your location

If you choose Use my location, your browser asks for permission and then gives the site your approximate coordinates. They are used only to fetch weather and a place name for that spot, as described below. We do not store or receive your location.

Services the site uses

To work, the site’s pages in your browser connect directly to these services, which receive your IP address and the details of each request:

  • Open-Meteo (weather data and place search): the coordinates, dates or search text needed for each request. Terms and privacy.
  • BigDataCloud (place names for “Use my location”): your coordinates. Privacy policy.
  • Esri (map tiles): the map area being viewed. Privacy statement.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting): standard web server logs. GitHub privacy statement.

Advertising and cookies

The site uses Google AdSense to show advertisements, which help keep it free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time from the privacy link shown with that message.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the site changes; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions about this policy can be raised on the project’s GitHub issues page.
